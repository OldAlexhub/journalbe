Innerfolio Privacy

Last updated October 18, 2026.

Local journal

Journal entries, plans, extracted items, and search indexes are stored in the encrypted vault on your Android device. Innerfolio has no consumer account and does not provide cloud synchronization.

Journal insights

Journal insights are enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings. While insights are enabled, saved entry text and selected context travel automatically over HTTPS to Old Alex Hub's server. The server reads that text temporarily to run conventional, non-generative language analysis. Text inputs and results are kept in bounded process memory, normally for no more than 15 minutes, and are discarded after acknowledgment or expiry. A server restart can discard an unfinished result. Operational records retain opaque installation and job identifiers, status, timings, and error codes—not journal text.

Voice

Dictation is processed on the device. The app does not save audio or upload voice recordings to the Innerfolio server.

Advertising

Eligible public guidance and template-library screens can show Google banner and native ads. Google and its consent SDK may process device, advertising, consent, diagnostic, and interaction data under Google's terms. Journal text, titles, people, plans, transcripts, searches, and analysis features are not supplied as ad targeting data.

Control

You may use the journal without server analysis, export readable data after a warning, create an encrypted backup, clear derived suggestions, or erase the vault. If the password and any configured biometric unlock become unavailable, the server cannot recover the local journal.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact published on Innerfolio's Google Play listing.
